🍼 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

Paternal Instinct ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your email address when you create an account via Firebase Authentication.
  • User Data: The "Estimated Due Date" you provide, along with any notes, tasks, or mood entries you create.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics to improve app performance and crash reports to fix bugs.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide personalized pregnancy content based on your due date
  • To store your tasks, notes, and mood entries securely
  • To improve app stability and user experience

4. Data Storage and Security

We use Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) to store and process your data. Firebase is a secure cloud platform provided by Google. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

5. Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.
How to delete: Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This action is permanent and immediate.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase - Authentication, database, analytics
  • Google Gemini AI - AI-powered Dad Coach feature

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for adults (expectant fathers) and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at: support@paternalinstinct.store
